## 2. WhatsApp Messaging Conduct
Because the Service sends messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform, Customer must:
- **Obtain valid opt-in consent** from each end customer before PHANTA sends that person any business-initiated WhatsApp message on Customer's behalf, clearly naming the business the person is opting in to hear from and the category of messages involved (transactional, reminders, or marketing), consistent with Meta's opt-in requirements and ECTA section 45's restrictions on unsolicited communications;
- Keep records of when and how that consent was obtained, and provide them to PHANTA on request if Meta or the Information Regulator queries a specific message. Where consent or an opt-out is captured through the Service, PHANTA records its source, method, and date on Customer's behalf;
- **Honour marketing opt-outs immediately.** Where an end customer opts out of marketing (for example by replying "STOP" or using the unsubscribe option on a marketing message), Customer must not send that person further marketing messages. The Service records the opt-out against the customer record and blocks marketing messages to that person automatically, including from scheduled and automated campaigns.
- Recognise that a marketing opt-out **does not** end the service relationship: booking confirmations, reminders, vehicle-ready notices, quotations, and invoices remain part of performing the service the end customer has requested, and continue to be sent. Customer must not use service message categories as a means of delivering marketing content to a person who has opted out of marketing;
- Not use the Service to send unsolicited bulk marketing, chain messages, or content unrelated to the workshop relationship;
- Not send messages regarding any "Regulated Vertical" restricted under Meta's Business Policy unless permitted in your jurisdiction and pre-cleared with PHANTA;
- Only use PHANTA-approved message templates for business-initiated conversations, as required by WhatsApp's template review process.

Repeated violations that risk PHANTA's or Customer's WhatsApp Business Account standing (quality rating, template rejections, or suspension) may result in immediate suspension of messaging features under clause 11 of the Terms of Service, independent of any other remedy.
