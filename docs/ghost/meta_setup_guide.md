# Setting up Meta (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and paid boosts)

This guide shows what **you, the workshop**, need to set up on Meta's side so PHANTA can:

- answer and book customers on **WhatsApp**
- post your specials to **Facebook** and **Instagram** (Flyer Lady)
- run **paid boosts** of your Facebook posts, paid from your own card

You can do the parts in any order. Each part takes about 5–15 minutes.

---

## Before you start: checklist

- A **Facebook account** that is an **admin** of the workshop's Facebook Page.
- A **Facebook Page** for the workshop (not a personal profile).
- A **Meta Business portfolio** (formerly Business Manager) at business.facebook.com. If you don't have one, Meta creates one during the WhatsApp setup.
- A **phone number for WhatsApp** that can receive an SMS or a call (see the warning below).
- A **bank card**, only needed for WhatsApp message charges and paid boosts.
- Your PHANTA login with **Owner** or **Manager** access.

> **Your WhatsApp number: two ways to connect**
> - **You already use the WhatsApp Business app** on your phone: keep it. Choose **"Connect your existing WhatsApp Business app"** in Meta's window. You keep chatting in the app, and the same chats show in PHANTA's **Inbox**. Your app needs to be up to date (version 2.24.17 or newer), and your contacts and last 6 months of chats are copied into PHANTA.
> - **A new number** (a new SIM or a landline that can receive a call): it works in PHANTA's **Inbox** only, not in a WhatsApp app on a phone.
> - A number on the normal (personal) WhatsApp app must first be moved to the **WhatsApp Business app** to keep using it on your phone.

## Part 1: Connect WhatsApp

1. In PHANTA, go to **Settings → WhatsApp** (or tap **WhatsApp** under Quick actions on the Dashboard).
2. Press **Connect WhatsApp**. Meta's window opens. If nothing happens, **allow pop-ups** for app.vantaautomations.co.za and press it again.
3. **Sign in** with the Facebook account that owns the workshop's business.
4. **Choose or create** your Business portfolio and WhatsApp Business account. Use your real business name.
5. Choose your number:
   - **Existing WhatsApp Business app:** choose **Connect your existing WhatsApp Business app**, enter the number, then open the WhatsApp Business app on your phone and **scan the QR code** Meta shows (or accept the message WhatsApp sends you). Keep the app open until Meta says it's done.
   - **New number:** add it, then enter the code Meta sends by SMS or call.
6. **Approve** the permissions PHANTA asks for and return to PHANTA.
7. If PHANTA asks for a **6-digit PIN** (new numbers only):
   - **New number:** make up any 6 digits and keep them somewhere safe.
   - **Number used with the WhatsApp API before:** use the PIN set back then.
8. The page should now say **Current connection: Connected** with your number.

**Test it:** send "Hi" to your WhatsApp number from another phone. It appears in **Inbox**, and the AI assistant replies. Its replies always start with "🤖 AI assistant:" so customers know it's not a person.

**Using the WhatsApp Business app too?** When you reply to a customer from the app on your phone, the reply shows in PHANTA's Inbox and the AI stops answering that customer, just like replying from the Inbox. Press **Hand back to AI** in the Inbox to let it continue. Open the WhatsApp Business app at least once every 14 days, or Meta disconnects it from PHANTA.

### Add a card for WhatsApp messages
Meta charges **your** WhatsApp account directly for some messages, mainly booking confirmations and reminders sent when the customer hasn't messaged you in the last 24 hours. Replies within 24 hours of a customer's message are free.

1. Go to business.facebook.com → **Billing & payments** (or open WhatsApp Manager → **Payment settings**).
2. Choose your **WhatsApp Business account** → **Add payment method** → add your card.

Without a card, those confirmation and reminder messages can't be sent.

### Message templates
Booking confirmations ("Reply YES to confirm"), reminders and "vehicle ready" messages use **templates approved by Meta**. PHANTA submits these for you, and Meta usually approves them within minutes to a few hours. You don't need to do anything.

## Part 2: Connect your Facebook Page (posting)

1. In PHANTA, open **Flyer Lady** → **Connections** → **Facebook** → **Connect**.
2. Sign in to Facebook and continue.
3. When Meta asks which **Pages** PHANTA may use, **tick your workshop's Page**. If you skip this, PHANTA won't see the Page.
4. Allow the permissions to **show Pages, read engagement and create posts**.
5. Back in PHANTA, **choose your Page** and confirm. **Facebook** now shows **Connected**.

**Someone else (an agency or friend) runs your Page?** You don't need to remove them. Ask them to give **your** Facebook account access to the Page with **permission to post** (Page settings → Page access, or Business settings → Pages → Assign people). Then do the steps above.

---

## Part 3: Connect Instagram

Instagram connects **separately** from Facebook.

1. Make sure your Instagram is a **professional account** (Business or Creator). In the Instagram app: **Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account**.
2. In PHANTA, open **Flyer Lady** → **Connections** → **Instagram** → **Connect**.
3. Sign in to **Instagram** (not Facebook) and press **Allow**.
4. PHANTA shows "**@yourname is connected to Flyer Lady**".

PHANTA renews the Instagram connection automatically. Note: links in Instagram captions can't be tapped, so Instagram posts build awareness but won't bring booking clicks the way Facebook posts do.

## Part 4: Paid boosts (adding your card)

Boosts are paid from **your own Meta ad account and your own card**. You pay Meta directly, and VANTA never handles your ad money.

### Step 1: Create an ad account
1. Go to business.facebook.com → **Settings** (Business settings) → **Accounts → Ad accounts** → **Add → Create a new ad account**.
2. Name it, e.g. "Smith's Auto – Ads".
3. Set **Time zone: Africa/Johannesburg** and **Currency: South African Rand (ZAR)**.
   ⚠️ Meta **can't change** these later, and PHANTA boosts only work with a **ZAR** ad account.
4. When asked who the ad account is for, choose **My business**.
5. Add **yourself** with **Full control** (Manage ad account).

### Step 2: Add your card
1. Go to business.facebook.com → **Billing & payments** → choose the **ad account** you just made.
2. Go to **Payment methods → Add payment method** and add your debit or credit card.
3. Optional: set an **account spending limit** as an extra safety net.

### Step 3: Keep the Page and ad account together
Make sure the **Facebook Page** and the **ad account** are in the **same Business portfolio** (Business settings → Accounts → Pages / Ad accounts).

### Step 4: Connect the ad account to PHANTA
1. In PHANTA, open **Flyer Lady → Connections → Facebook ads (paid boosts)** → **Connect ad account**.
2. Sign in and allow the **ads** permissions.
3. If you have more than one ad account, **choose the workshop's one**. PHANTA shows "**Ad account … is connected**".

> If it says **"Not set up"** instead of a Connect button, boosting isn't switched on for your workshop yet. Ask VANTA.

### Step 5: Boost a special
1. **Create** a special in Flyer Lady, **Approve** it, and **Queue** it to the **Facebook feed**. Within about 5 minutes it's published.
2. Under the published special, fill in:
   - **Budget (R):** the most you'll spend in total, R50 to R20,000.
   - **Days:** 1 to 30.
   - **Town / city:** e.g. Pretoria.
   - **Radius (km):** 17 to 80 km around that town.
3. Press **Boost on Facebook** and confirm the amount.
4. **Meta reviews the ad**, usually within 24 hours, and then it starts running.
5. Press **Results** on the boost to see what has been spent, how many people saw it, and how many clicked.

The ad shows your published post to people aged 18 and over around the town you chose. Meta **never spends more than your budget**, and the ad stops after the number of days you set.

---

## Seeing what it brings in

Open **Results** in the sidebar (Owners and Managers only) to see, for the last 7, 30 or 90 days:

- **Bookings brought in**, by source: Flyer Lady posts, your booking page, and the WhatsApp AI assistant.
- **Per special:** link clicks, bookings, and boost budget.

Every post ends with a **"Book here"** link. Bookings made through it are counted against that special.

## Problems and fixes

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nothing happens when pressing **Connect WhatsApp** | Allow pop-ups for app.vantaautomations.co.za, then try again. |
| "WhatsApp setup is not finished yet" | Enter your 6-digit PIN and press **Finish setup**, or leave the PIN empty and press it to retry. |
| My number no longer works in the WhatsApp app | It was connected as a new number. To keep using the app, it must be connected with **Connect your existing WhatsApp Business app**. Contact VANTA support. |
| "Your number isn't eligible" for the WhatsApp Business app option | Update the WhatsApp Business app, make sure the number is a Business app number (not personal WhatsApp), then try again. |
| "Meta didn't return any Facebook Pages" | Connect again and **tick your Page** in Meta's window. You must be an admin of the Page. |
| Instagram connection fails | Switch Instagram to a **Business or Creator** account, then connect again. |
| "Meta didn't return an active ad account" | Create the ad account (Part 4, Step 1) and add a card, then connect again. |
| "Your ad account uses USD…" | Create a new **ZAR** ad account and connect that one. |
| "Meta refused the boost: …no payment method" | Add a card to the ad account (Part 4, Step 2). |
| "Connect your Meta ad account first" | Do Part 4, Step 4. |
| "Publish this special to the Facebook feed first" | Queue it with **facebook feed** ticked and wait until it shows as published. |
| WhatsApp shows **Reconnect** | Go to Settings → WhatsApp → **Reconnect WhatsApp** and sign in again. |

Still stuck? Contact VANTA support and include a screenshot of the message you see.
