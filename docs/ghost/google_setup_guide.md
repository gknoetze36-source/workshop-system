# Setting up Google Ads (Search ads for your specials)

This guide shows what **you, the workshop**, need to set up on Google's side so PHANTA can run your Flyer Lady specials as **Google Search ads**. These are the "Sponsored" results people see when they search things like *"car service Pretoria"* or *"brake repairs near me"*.

Ads are paid from **your own Google Ads account and your own card**. You pay Google directly, and VANTA never handles your ad money.

Time needed: about 15–20 minutes, once.

---

## Before you start: checklist

- A **Google account**, either Gmail or your business email on Google.
- A **bank card** for Google Ads.
- Your PHANTA login with **Owner** or **Manager** access.
- Recommended (free): a **Google Business Profile** for the workshop (see Tips).

---

## Step 1: Create your Google Ads account

1. Go to **ads.google.com** and sign in with your Google account.
2. Google will try to walk you through making your first campaign. **You don't need to.** Look for **"Switch to Expert Mode"**, then **"Create an account without a campaign"**. Google moves these links around from time to time. If you can't find them, finish the guided setup and then **pause** the campaign it made.
3. Confirm your business details:
   - **Billing country:** South Africa
   - **Time zone:** (GMT+02:00) Johannesburg
   - **Currency:** **South African Rand (ZAR)**

   ⚠️ Google **can't change** time zone and currency later, and PHANTA only works with a **ZAR** account.
4. Write down your **Customer ID**: the 10-digit number at the top of Google Ads, e.g. **123-456-7890**.

## Step 2: Add your card

1. In Google Ads, open **Billing → Settings** (or **Billing → Payment methods**).
2. Choose **automatic payments** and add your **debit or credit card**.
3. If Google asks you to **verify your business or identity** (advertiser verification), complete it. Your ads may be limited until you do.

## Step 3: Connect Google Ads to PHANTA

1. In PHANTA, open **Flyer Lady → Connections → Google Ads** → **Connect Google Ads**.
2. Sign in with the **same Google account** you used in Step 1. It must have **Admin** or **Standard** access to the Google Ads account.
3. Press **Allow** when Google asks if PHANTA may manage your Google Ads campaigns.
4. If you have more than one Google Ads account, **choose the workshop's one**. Check the 10-digit Customer ID.
5. PHANTA shows "**Google Ads is connected (account 123-456-7890)**".

> If it says **"Not set up"** instead of a Connect button, Google Ads isn't switched on for your workshop yet. Ask VANTA.

## Step 4: Run a special as a Google Search ad

1. In **Flyer Lady**, **create** a special and **Approve** it.
2. In the list of places to publish, tick **google search ad**. Four boxes appear:
   - **Budget (R):** the most you'll spend in total, R50 to R20,000.
   - **Days:** 1 to 30. Google spreads the budget evenly over these days.
   - **Town / city:** e.g. Pretoria.
   - **Radius (km):** 17 to 80 km around that town.
3. Press **Queue selected**.
4. **Google reviews the ad**, usually within one working day. Then it starts showing.

### What PHANTA sets up for you
- **Where it shows:** **Google Search** only. No banners on other websites and no YouTube.
- **Who sees it:** only people searching within your radius.
- **Searches it shows up for:** *car service, vehicle service, car repairs, mechanic, car maintenance, brake repairs, service my car*, including longer searches that contain these words, like "cheap car service in Pretoria".
- **Ad text:** written from your special and workshop name, with several headlines and 3 descriptions. Google mixes them for each search, usually showing 2–3 headlines and 1–2 descriptions.
- **Bidding:** set to get **as many clicks as possible** within your budget.
- **Where a tap goes:** your booking page, through your special's tracking link, so every click and booking is counted on your **Results** page.

## Example

**Your special in Flyer Lady:**

> R999 Brake Special
> Front brake pads fitted plus a free safety check. Book before Friday.

**Budget R200 · 5 days · Pretoria · 25 km**

**Roughly how the ad looks on a phone:**

> **Sponsored**
> **Smith's Auto Workshop** · app.vantaautomations.co.za
> **R999 Brake Special | Smith's Auto Workshop | Book Your Car Service Online**
> R999 Brake Special Front brake pads fitted plus a free safety check. Book before Friday. Book online with Smith's Auto Workshop in under a minute.

**What R200 might buy:** Google charges per click, not per view. Clicks for car-repair searches in South Africa have been estimated at roughly **R8 to R18** each, so R200 over 5 days is **roughly 10 to 25 visits** to your booking page. Treat this as a guide only. The real price depends on your area and how many other workshops are advertising. Your actual spend and clicks show under **Results**.

## Tips for better results

- **Make the first line of your special short and punchy (30 characters or less).** It becomes the ad headline. Good: "R999 Brake Special". Too long: "This week only we have an amazing brake special…"
- **Keep the next sentence under about 90 characters.** It becomes the ad description.
- **Use a price or a clear offer.** Searchers compare quickly, and "R999" or "Free safety check" stands out.
- **Run at least 5–7 days.** Google needs a few days to learn who clicks.
- **Set up your free Google Business Profile** at **business.google.com**: address, opening hours, phone, photos, and ask happy customers for **reviews**. It helps you show on **Google Maps** and builds trust when people see your ad.

---

## Seeing what it brings in

Open **Results** in the sidebar to see bookings brought in, link clicks and bookings per special. On each Google ad, press **Results** to see the **amount spent** and the **clicks** so far.

## Problems and fixes

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This Google login has no Google Ads account" | Create one (Step 1), add a card (Step 2), then connect again. |
| "…more than one Google Ads account. Reconnect Google Ads and pick the workshop's account" | Press **Connect Google Ads** again and choose the right account. |
| "Your Google Ads account uses USD…" | Create a new Google Ads account in **ZAR** and connect that one. |
| "Google Ads needs reconnecting" | Press **Connect Google Ads** again and sign in. |
| "Google refused the ad: …" | Read Google's reason. Usually a missing card, unfinished verification, or wording Google doesn't allow. Fix it in Google Ads and try again. |
| "Connect Google Ads first" | Do Step 3. |
| The ad shows "Under review" for days | Check Google Ads for messages about **advertiser verification** or **billing**. |
| "…only has Test access to the Google Ads API" | This is on VANTA's side. Contact VANTA support. |
| Your ad account is run by an agency (manager account) | Contact VANTA before connecting. This setup isn't supported yet. |

Still stuck? Contact VANTA support and include a screenshot of the message you see.
