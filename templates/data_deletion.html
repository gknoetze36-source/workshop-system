User Data Deletion

VANTA Automations respects your right to request deletion of personal data associated with our services and Meta-connected features.

How Meta-connected deletion works

If you remove a Meta connection and Meta sends VANTA Automations a valid data deletion request, our secure callback verifies the request and removes Meta-connected data that can be reliably associated with that Meta user.

What may be deleted

  • Stored Flyer Lady Facebook Page and Instagram connection details associated with the requesting Meta user.
  • Stored Meta social access credentials associated with that connection.
  • Temporary Meta social OAuth session credentials associated with the matched connection.

What is not automatically deleted

Business records that cannot safely be identified from Meta's app-scoped user ID are not guessed or deleted automatically. This prevents one person's Meta request from deleting the wrong workshop, customer, or business record.

Need to request deletion directly?

Email info@vantaautomations.co.za with the subject User Data Deletion Request and include enough information for us to identify the relevant account safely.

Why we do this: We provide a clear, accessible process for deletion requests and minimise the risk of deleting data belonging to the wrong person or business.
