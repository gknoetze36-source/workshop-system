Role Descriptions

Understanding the different roles and their permissions

Owner

Full access to all settings, billing, and team management. Can transfer ownership.

Manager

Can manage appointments, services, and team members. Has access to most settings except billing and ownership transfer.

Reception

Can manage appointments and customers. Limited access to settings and reports.

Technician

Can view and update job statuses. Limited customer and scheduling access.

Read Only

Can view appointments, customers, and reports. Cannot make any changes.
