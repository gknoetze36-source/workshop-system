PHANTA ARCHITECTURE

System boundaries

These are documented product boundaries, not live health claims.

01

WhatsApp Messages

Customer communication through the Meta WhatsApp integration. It remains separate from Flyer Lady and Service Advisor.

02

Service Advisor

Vehicle/service intelligence and deterministic maintenance guidance. It does not become the workshop pricing or repair-authorisation engine.

03

Flyer Lady

Public promotion and publishing. It must not alter, replace or interfere with WhatsApp messaging or Service Advisor.

04

Location isolation

Workshop data and credentials remain location-scoped.
