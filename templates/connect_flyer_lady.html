PHANTA will open Meta's secure signup window. You will choose your workshop's Facebook Page there, and PHANTA will use it to publish specials to Facebook and Instagram.

  1. Sign in to the Facebook account that manages the workshop's Page.
  2. Select the Page you want Flyer Lady to post from.
  3. If you post to Instagram too, make sure that account is linked to the Page.
  4. Approve the permissions, then pick your Page on the screen that follows.
