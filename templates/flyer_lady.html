How it works

  1. Submit the workshop's text and optional image.
  2. Approve the special.
  3. Post for free: tick where it goes (Facebook Feed, Facebook Story, Instagram, WhatsApp Status) and press Post selected.
  4. Paid promotion (optional): tick Facebook ad and/or Google search ad, choose your total amount, 1 week or 1 month, and how to split it. Meta and Google charge your own cards, never more than your amount.
  5. PHANTA publishes approved content through the publishing queue and prepares the WhatsApp Status asset for manual sharing.

WhatsApp Status remains a prepare-and-share action because Meta does not expose an official Status publishing endpoint.
