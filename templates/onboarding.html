Quick Start Guide

Follow these steps to get your workshop fully operational

  1. 1. Business Information

    Set up your workshop's basic details including name, address, contact information, and timezone.

  2. 2. Services Configuration

    Define the services you offer, their duration, pricing, and availability.

  3. 3. WhatsApp Setup

    Connect your WhatsApp Business Account to enable automated customer communication.

  4. 4. Automation Setup

    Configure automated appointment reminders, follow-ups, and other workflows.

  5. 5. Team Setup

    Invite team members and assign appropriate roles and permissions.

  6. 6. Review & Complete

    Review all your settings and complete the onboarding process.
