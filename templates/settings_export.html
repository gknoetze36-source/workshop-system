Export Your Data

Download a copy of your workshop's records held by PHANTA.

The export is a JSON file containing your customers, vehicles, bookings, service records, quotes, invoices, conversations and messages, along with your consent and legal acceptance records.

Integration credentials and PHANTA's internal system logs are not included — they are not your business records, and a file containing access tokens should not be downloadable.

The file contains your customers' personal information. Store it somewhere secure and delete it when you no longer need it. Every export is recorded in your audit log.
